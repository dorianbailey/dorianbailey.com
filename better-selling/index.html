Better Selling With Bailey

Estate sales in Erie County, run start to finish by one person, so the people selling keep the proceeds.

Better Selling With Bailey prices, stages, promotes and runs estate sales in the client’s own home, then clears whatever is left at no charge. Many clients treat it as a cleanout of a garage, basement or collection that happens to pay.

Every client works with me directly, from the first walkthrough to the last sale of the day. I set the prices, arrange the rooms, write the listings, run the floor and handle the teardown.

A Star Wars collector’s sale in North East drew a crowd through heavy rain and wind in May 2026, and was covered by YourErie.com.
