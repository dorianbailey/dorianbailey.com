What it is

A full-service estate sale company hosted at the client's home. Every sale is handled start to finish: pricing and organization, staging, promotion, day-of management, and cleanup.

Clients work directly with me from the first consultation to the final day of the sale. It's a personal, one-on-one experience in an industry where clients are often passed between employees.

My role

  • Founder and sole point of contact for every client
  • Strategic pricing of collections from LEGO and Star Wars to antiques and tools
  • Marketing and promotion: website, Facebook events, local press
  • Day-of sales, customer service and event coordination
