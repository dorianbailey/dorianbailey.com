How a sale works

Every sale is hosted at the client’s home in Erie County and handled start to finish.

  1. Walkthrough and pricing

    I assess everything in the home and price it to sell, with you as involved in the decisions as you like.

  2. Staging

    Rooms are organized and merchandise is laid out so buyers can browse easily and find what they came for.

  3. Promotion

    Listings, Facebook events and local outreach bring the right buyers to the door, whatever the weather.

  4. Sale days

    I run the floor: every customer, every sale and every question. You don’t need to be there unless you want to be.

  5. Cleanup

    Teardown at the end of the sale, and free removal of whatever is left if you want it gone.

  6. Proceeds

    The earnings go to you, minus the flat fee and commission agreed before the sale began.
