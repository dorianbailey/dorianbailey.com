I

Green Eyes

Green Eyes Festival, Chickamauga, Georgia, October 2026. 15 plates.

A day of cryptids, costumes and card tables at a festival named for the battlefield legend of Ole’ Green Eyes.

II

Letchworth

Letchworth State Park, New York, October 2026. 9 plates.

The Genesee River gorge in early autumn: high falls, a railroad bridge, a passing rainbow, and the friends on the trail.

III

North East

North East, Pennsylvania, September–October 2026. 12 plates.

Home: Halloween porches after dark, the park in full color, and the Wine Fest run through town.

IV

Creatures

Pennsylvania, September–October 2026. 14 plates.

Cats in window light, a poodle at the water bowl, horses, goats, and one woolly bear crossing the leaves.

V

Studies

Penn State Behrend and elsewhere, September–October 2026. 9 plates.

Close looks at small things: fungi on fallen wood, asters, a weed in shale, and an eye.
