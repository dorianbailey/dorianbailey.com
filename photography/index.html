Marketing assistant since June 2026

Vanta House

A video-first content studio in North East, Pennsylvania, that turns one long-form film a month into a full system of content for its clients. I support content strategy, social campaigns and SEO across client accounts, and work alongside the team on shoots and video production.

Approach

Most of this is event work: long days on a festival street or a tournament floor, close to the people and the cards.

The kit is small on purpose. Two fast primes for faces and details, a zoom for when the crowd closes in, and everything edited in Lightroom. The personal collections are where the slower pictures live: a gorge in October, a cat in window light, fungi on a fallen log.
