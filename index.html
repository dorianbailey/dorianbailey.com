Foreword

I’m a lifelong resident of North East, Pennsylvania, and a student of Marketing and Management Information Systems at Penn State Behrend.

Long before I started a business, I spent years buying, selling, researching and pricing things of every kind. That became Better Selling With Bailey. A taste for the strange side of the map became Take The OddWay, and a love of cryptid lore and collecting became Bailey MetaZoo. Somewhere along the way I picked up a camera.

This site gathers all of it in one place: the work I’m paid for and the work I’d do anyway.
