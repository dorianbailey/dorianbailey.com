Foreword

Born and raised in North East, Pennsylvania, I study Marketing and Management Information Systems at Penn State Behrend.

Long before I started a business, I spent years buying, selling, researching and pricing things of every kind. That became Better Selling With Bailey. A taste for the strange side of the map became Take The OddWay, and a love of cryptid lore and collecting became Bailey MetaZoo. Somewhere along the way I picked up a camera, and now photograph events for MetaZoo TCG.

By day I’m a marketing assistant at Vanta House, a video-first content studio here in North East. This site gathers the rest: the work I’m paid for and the work I’d do anyway.
