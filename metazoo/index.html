Bailey MetaZoo

MetaZoo trading card content: pack openings, collecting, and retro VHS-style card reveals.

What it is

MetaZoo is a trading card game built around cryptids, folklore and the paranormal. Bailey MetaZoo is where I share openings, pulls and my collection.

My signature format is the VHS reveal: a tape slides into a VCR, the CRT flickers on, and the cards flash through static before the hero card lands.

My role

  • Creator, editor and host
  • Short-form vertical video production
  • Collecting, grading and selling
